🗺️ Itinerary PDF Generator

A React-based travel itinerary builder that lets you create, preview, and export beautifully designed trip itineraries as PDF files — all within your browser! ✨

You can:

1.Add your own trip title, subtitle, traveler name, and travel dates

2.Add or remove daily activities (with images and descriptions)

3.Instantly preview the itinerary in real-time

4.Export your itinerary as a print-ready PDF

🚀 Features

✅ Live Preview – See your itinerary update as you type
✅ Add Unlimited Days – Easily add or remove trip days
✅ Beautiful Layout – Clean, modern, and printer-friendly design
✅ PDF Export – One click to generate a high-quality PDF
✅ Image Support – Each day can include a scenic photo
✅ Responsive UI – Works smoothly on desktops and tablets

🧰 Tech Stack

React.js – Frontend framework

Tailwind CSS – For styling and responsive design

jsPDF – To generate PDFs

html2canvas – To capture the live preview area as an image for the PDF

🧑‍💻 Usage

1)Enter your trip title, subtitle, and traveler name

2)Select start and end dates

3)Add your daily activities with headings, details, and image URLs

4)Preview your itinerary instantly on the right panel

5)Click “Get Itinerary” to download it as a PDF

6)(Optional) Click “Print” to get a physical copy
